Module 4
Agent control panel
What it is doing

Flag P0 risks from #escalations before they reach leadership.

Rules of engagement

Agency Permission:

Agent can draft a P0 risk summary and Jira stub. Agent CANNOT auto-close threads or DM customers.

Access Control:

READ: Slack #escalations, Strategy KB, Salesforce ARR. WRITE: #pm-daily only, Jira stubs only. CANNOT edit Salesforce or post outside #pm-daily.

Fallback Protocols:

After 3 failed retrievals, degrade to "cautious mode" (no priorities, just thread links). After 2 tool errors, escalate to PM with full trace.

Checkpoints:

Any thread mentioning "churn", "legal", or "security" requires PM approval. Any P0 where Juno is low confidence goes to PM review.
